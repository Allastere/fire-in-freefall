METHODOLOGY & SPREAD REGIMES

Parametric Boundaries in Low-Speed Flow

Physical Sciences Informatics (PSI) system compilation for microgravity fuel droplet combustion. Radiative extinction thresholds correlate directly with low convective transport limits, preventing flame stabilization below critical Peclet numbers.

In typical terrestrial buoyancy (1.0 g), upward natural convection flushes combustion products and stabilizes steady diffusion envelopes. In orbital freefall (10⁻⁶ g), the convective velocity drops to near zero, creating thick, spherical diffusion flames. When an artificial laminar flow field (U_∞) is introduced within the Combustion Integrated Rack, radiative heat loss from soot and combustion products can exceed chemical heat production at low speeds, causing abrupt radiative quench prior to complete mass depletion.
